What actually helps

You need boundaries.

1

Define “done” before you start.

Set the scope up front. Stop when you hit it.

2

Track what you eliminate, not what you produce.

Measure time saved, not tasks completed.

3

Push back on scope creep.

When someone says “but with AI you can just...”, the answer is “yes, and I’ve already allocated that capacity elsewhere.”

4

Use efficiency gains for recovery, not more work.

If AI saves you two hours, spend one of them not working.

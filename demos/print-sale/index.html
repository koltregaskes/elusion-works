Frames

Black, oak or white, each with a card mount. A frame adds 14 cm to the width and to the height. A4 +£30, A3 +£45, A2 +£70. Frames are not in the sale.

Postage

£4.50 for an order of rolled prints, sent in a tube. £14 when anything in it is framed. Free on orders of £150 or more.

Paper and tax

A real shop would say here what the prints are made on, such as pigment inks on heavy cotton paper, and whether its prices include VAT. This one is a mock, so there is no paper and no tax.

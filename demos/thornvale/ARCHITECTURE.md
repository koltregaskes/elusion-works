# Thornvale

A walled medieval hill town you can explore on foot as a third-person
character, fly over, or watch as a looping flyover. It has a day and night
cycle and four kinds of weather. It runs in the browser on three.js r187 with
the WebGPU renderer, and falls back to WebGL2.

An Unreal Engine 5 route for the same town is in `unreal/`.

## How it is made

| Part | Where | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assets | `tools/build_kit.py` (headless Blender) | Half-timbered houses, walls, towers, gatehouse, keep, arcade wall, church, market and street props, trees |
| Textures | `tools/build_textures.py` (Blender's Python) | 13 tileable PBR sets plus a leaf atlas |
| Terrain | `src/terrain.js` | One height function: plateau, terrace, glacis, road, hills; snowy mountain ring |
| Layout | `src/town.js` | Walls and towers on an ellipse, terrace, church, keep, square, houses along streets, forest |
| Rendering | `src/world.js`, `src/kit.js`, `src/main.js` | Physical sky with clouds, sun with cascaded shadows, image-based light from the sky, terrain splatting, instanced grass, rain, GTAO, bloom, TRAA |
| Character | `src/character.js` | Mixamo character (from the three.js examples) with idle, walk and run, a follow camera and collisions |

### The Blender kit

- **Houses (10 variants):** stone ground floors with quoins, plinths, doors
  under stone lintels, shop windows and shuttered windows. Jettied upper
  floors are built as real timber frames: sole and top plates, posts, rails,
  braces and St Andrew's crosses, with plaster infill set back between them.
  Windows are recessed, mullioned and transomed with leaded panes, shutters
  and flower boxes. Steep gable roofs (clay pantile or slate) have barge
  boards, rafter ends, ridge caps and framed gables with attic windows; there
  are dormers, chimneys with pots, door lanterns and hanging shop signs.
- **Fortifications:** 4 m crenellated wall sections with a battered plinth,
  string course, arrow slits and a walkway; round towers with machicolation
  corbels (crenellated or with conical slate roofs); a gatehouse with half-
  round towers, a pointed arch, portcullis, timber hoarding and banners; a
  square keep with bartizans; an arcaded retaining wall for the terrace.
- **Church:** six buttressed bays with lancet windows, a slate roof, an apse,
  twin west towers with belfry openings, octagonal spires and pinnacles, a
  pointed portal and a rose window with tracery.
- **Props:** market stalls with striped sagging awnings and produce, barrels,
  crates, a hay cart, a well, a tiered fountain, lamp posts, benches,
  planters, banner poles, hay bales and fences.
- **Trees:** branching oaks with leaf cards (green, autumn orange and gold
  from one atlas, chosen per tree), cypresses, two pines and bushes.
  Leaves sway in the wind in the shader.

### Lighting and weather

- `SkyMesh` (physical sky with clouds) drives the sky and, through a cube
  camera, the image-based lighting, so the ambient light follows the time of
  day and the cloud cover.
- The sun's arc runs from dawn to dusk. Night brings moonlight, lit windows,
  lantern glow, and the nearest lanterns as real point lights.
- Weather: clear, cloudy, rain (rain streaks, wet darker and glossier
  surfaces, stronger wind), and mist (thick fog).

## Controls

- **1 Explore**: WASD to walk, mouse to turn the camera, Shift to run, wheel to zoom.
- **2 Fly**: free camera, Space/E up and Q/C down.
- **3 Flyover**: seven shots on a loop, N and B to skip.
- **T** time of day, **R** weather, **H** help.

URL options: `?mode=walk|fly|flyover`, `?time=dawn|midday|golden|night`,
`?weather=clear|cloudy|rain|mist`, `?q=low|high|ultra`, `?shot=N`, `?aa=fxaa`.

## Rebuild the assets

```bash
B=/path/to/python-with-bpy   # uv venv + uv pip install bpy
$B -I demos/thornvale/tools/build_kit.py demos/thornvale/assets/thornvale-kit.glb
$B -I demos/thornvale/tools/build_textures.py /tmp/tvtex
for f in /tmp/tvtex/*.png; do ffmpeg -y -i $f -quality 90 demos/thornvale/assets/tex/$(basename $f .png).webp; done
ffmpeg -y -i /tmp/tvtex/leaves.png -pix_fmt yuva420p -quality 92 demos/thornvale/assets/tex/leaves.webp
```

## Checks

`tools/capture.mjs` serves the repo, opens the demo in headless Chromium
(software rendering on the WebGL2 fallback), reports errors and saves frames,
including a walking shot of the character. Software rendering takes minutes
a frame, so its numbers say nothing about real-GPU speed.

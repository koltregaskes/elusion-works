Thornvale

A walled hill town with an upper terrace, a church and a keep. Every house, wall, tower and tree was modelled in Blender by Claude from a script; the textures were generated too. It is rendered live with three.js on WebGPU.

Explore
WASD walk, drag (or click to lock the mouse) to look, Shift runs, wheel zooms
Fly
as Explore, plus Space/E up and Q/C down
Flyover
N next shot, B previous
World
T time of day, R weather
Touch
drag the left side to move, the right side to look

Character and animations: the Mixamo soldier from the three.js examples. Quality: add #q=ultra to the address for a strong GPU, #q=low for laptops and phones.

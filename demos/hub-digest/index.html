What an email has to pass

    
Each rule reads the finished email itself: every tag, and every piece of text with the colour, background and size it ends up with. They are rules, not a test in Outlook or Gmail.

    
    

  • ✓Small enough not to be cut off (passes)21.5 KB as written. Gmail cuts an email off above 102.0 KB, and a sending service adds to it.
    • 

  • ✓A subject that fits an inbox (passes)58 characters, and 60 is about what a phone shows.
    • 

  • ✓A hidden line to follow the subject (passes)108 characters, first in the email and hidden from the reader, for the inbox to show.
    • 

  • ✓No pictures (passes)Many inboxes hide pictures until asked. There are none, in the tags or the styles.
    • 

  • ✓No script, form or frame (passes)No tag that runs or collects anything, no handler on any tag, no link that runs code.
    • 

  • ✓Nothing fetched from elsewhere (passes)No web fonts, no style sheets, no tracking pixel.
    • 

  • ✓Every link is a full, secure address or left for the sender (passes)10 links, each with words to press.
    • 

  • ✓A way to stop the emails, and a place for who sent them (passes)Both are in the words a reader sees. Whether a send meets the law is for the sender to check.
    • 

  • ✓Layout by tables that screen readers skip (passes)13 tables, each marked as layout only.
    • 

  • ✓No flex, grid or positioning (passes)In the tags and the style block alike. Tables, padding and plain colours are what email programs share.
    • 

  • ✓Text large enough to read on a phone (passes)Every piece of text was measured: 14 px or more, and 12 px for labels in capitals.
    • 

  • ✓Every piece of text is easy to read against what is behind it (passes)Worked out for each piece of text from its own colour and background: the weakest is 7.9 to 1, and 4.5 is the mark. 9 pairings are used.
    • 

  • ✓The language is said twice, and the title is the subject (passes)Once on the page and once on the email itself, because webmail throws the first away.
    • 

  • ✓A plain-text version that says the same (passes)91 lines. Every piece of text and every link in the email is in it, and no line is over 78 characters but for a link.

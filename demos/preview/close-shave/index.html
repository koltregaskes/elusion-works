Ready?

Move to the gap before the wall reaches you. Through with almost no room to spare is a close shave, and raises what the next wall is worth. It needs sight and quick hands.

This game needs scripts. With them off there is nothing to play.

How to play

  • MoveArrow keys or W A S D. On a touch screen, drag anywhere in the room.
  • CreepHold Shift to move slowly, for cutting it fine.
  • PauseP or Esc, or the button. The game also pauses by itself when you look away.
  • StartSpace or Enter.

Scoring

  • A wall10 points, times your run.
  • A close shaveThrough with almost no room to spare, by the edges marked green. Double points, and your run goes up by one, to five at most.
  • A wide berthYour run drops by one.
  • A hitCosts one of three lives and puts your run back to one. The game ends when the lives are gone.

Every wall can be got through, from wherever you are. Each is shown at the edge before it comes in, and its gap is never further from where you stand than you can go in that warning. Nothing is saved: your best score lasts until you leave the page.

An original game made for Elusion Works. It needs sight and quick hands: there is no way to play it by screen reader.

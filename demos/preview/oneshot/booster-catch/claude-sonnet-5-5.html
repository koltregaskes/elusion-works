Credits, sources & rights

Event depicted: Starship Flight 5 (IFT-5), 13 Oct 2024, Starbase, Texas — first catch of a Super Heavy booster by the launch-tower “chopstick” arms.

Mission / footage owner: Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX). Starship, Super Heavy, Mechazilla and related names are SpaceX's. This project is independent, unofficial, educational, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SpaceX.

What this file contains: only original, procedurally generated geometry, textures, shaders and animation written for this reconstruction. No SpaceX footage, photographs, audio, logos, livery or CAD are embedded or loaded. Proportions (≈71 m booster, 9 m diameter, 33 engines, ≈146 m tower, two catch arms) are from public descriptions; the design details are my own approximations.

Reference rights: The three REF event times were transcribed from SpaceX's published Flight 5 mission timeline / webcast (13 Oct 2024). Timing facts are used for citation only. If you compare against SpaceX's own video, or want to embed it or any of their imagery, you must clear that use with SpaceX separately — nothing here grants such rights.

Verification status:

What is modelled, not measured: trajectory, speeds, altitudes, attitude, grid-fin motion, arm timing, engine-count transitions, camera positions, dust and plume. All camera views are virtual and are not the positions of any real camera. Everything not labelled REF is APPROX.

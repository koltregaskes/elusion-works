THIS WEEK

ASK IT HOW IT KNOWS

The most useful habit we picked up this month costs nothing. After a
model gives you an answer, ask it which parts it worked out and which
parts it is recalling. The answer is not always right, but it tells you
where to look first.

Two of this week’s resources are built on that idea. The dataset shows
its working for every answer, and the guide shows you how to test a
model on questions of your own. The other two keep your work on your own
machine.

NEW ON THE HUB

Lanternfile (Tool)
Turns a folder of PDFs into a notebook you can question, and runs on
your own machine so nothing leaves it.
Good for: anyone with a pile of reports and no time.

Evals without the jargon (Guide)
A forty-minute read on testing a model against your own questions, with

https://theairesourcehub.com/#evals-without-the-jargon

Kittiwake 2 Small (Model)

Twelve hundred questions with checked answers and the working shown, for

Good for: people who build things on top of models.

out, recalled or guessed. For anything recalled or guessed, tell me how
I could check it.

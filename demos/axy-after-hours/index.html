      About this page

      An experimental homepage for Axy Lusion, Kol Tregaskes's AI art, video and music archive. Everything you see is real HTML: the headline is a heading, the buttons are links, the slider is a slider. Where the browser supports HTML-in-Canvas, the live page is drawn into a canvas and passed through a shader, so the text itself bends around the well and the gallery wall catches the lamplight.

      Two rooms

      Undertow. A gravity well sits in the page. Drag it, raise its mass, or feed it and the page falls in piece by piece. Focus anything with Tab and it climbs back out.

      Lamplight. The gallery after closing. Move three lamps over a wall of frames. Each lamp has brightness and warmth, and the house lights can come up.

      Without HTML-in-Canvas

      You get the same page as ordinary HTML. Things still fall and the lamps still light the wall, but the text does not bend. The status line at the bottom says which version you have.

      The frames

      The frames shown here are generative tone studies, drawn in your browser from the colour record of real frames in the Axy Lusion archive. They stand in for the archive images, which are not bundled with this experiment. As on the archive itself, frames carry a queue reference and no title.

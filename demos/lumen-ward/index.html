Lumen Ward

A small rain-soaked district, about 240 metres across. Every building and prop was modelled in Blender by Claude from a script, then laid out and lit here in Three.js.

Walk
WASD move, mouse looks, Shift runs
Fly
as Walk, plus Space/E up and Q/C down
Flyover
N next shot, B previous
Touch
drag left side to move, right side to look

Add ?q=low to the address for slower machines (no street reflections).

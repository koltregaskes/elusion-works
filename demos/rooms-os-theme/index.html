house theme

Charcoal, white ink and one green, with a faint floor plan behind the conversation whose rooms light up in turn. It is a small mod for Claude Code: load it, and a session drawn in the Claude desktop or mobile app takes on the look of the house.

The colours

The theme is made from eighteen colours: seven for the night half (four surfaces and three inks), six for the day half, and five roles shared by both. The apps’ own colour maker turns those into about a hundred for each half. Every ratio below is worked out by this page from the colours shown, and cut short, never rounded up.

Each colour, by night and by day. A ratio is how well it reads against the page: 4.5 to 1 is the usual bar for words, 7 to 1 for comfortable reading.

The colour maker’s notes on the day half

    The green you give is a starting point. The apps keep its hue and choose their own lightness for each job, so buttons come out a deeper green than #3fe08a. Forcing the exact green onto buttons was tried and left out: a white switch knob then reads at only 1.7 to 1 against it.

    Trying it

    The mod is the folder demos/rooms-os-theme/mod in the Elusion Works repository. Themes are drawn by the Claude desktop and mobile apps; a terminal draws none, and the mod says so in the status line instead.

    For a session started from a terminal, load the folder with a flag. It lasts for that session only, and nothing is installed:

    claude --plugin-dir demos/rooms-os-theme/mod

    For sessions the desktop app starts there is no flag to give. Claude Code reads the same folders from a setting instead: the folder’s full path, under CLAUDE_CODE_PLUGIN_DIRS in the env block of ~/.claude/settings.json. Take the line out again and the look is gone.

    • Not yet seen in the apps. The mod passes Claude Code’s own validator, its type check and its tests, which draw the scene through the engine on the desktop and mobile surfaces. Nobody has yet looked at it painted in a real app, in either half of the day.
    • Early access. Themes for the apps are new and the way they are written may change between releases. The colour maker is copied from the toolkit unchanged and will need copying again when the toolkit moves on.
    • Permission prompts keep the apps’ own look. That is the apps’ rule, not this theme’s choice.
    After a post by ClaudeDevs. An original theme by Elusion Works; not made by or with Anthropic. Nothing is loaded from anywhere else and nothing is stored.

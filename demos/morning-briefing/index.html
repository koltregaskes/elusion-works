This is a sample. The day, the jobs, the sites, the costs and the list are made up for this demonstration. No real system is read.

The briefing is read aloud only by a voice your device says is its own. If it offers none, or only voices that may send the words to a service elsewhere, it is read along in silence at the same pace, and the page says why. Which voices are the device's own is the device's word; the page cannot check it. The page itself stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

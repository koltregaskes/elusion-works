    About Stem Studio

    A small arrangement room that runs in the browser. Four synth tracks play a 32-bar song written for this page. Move, resize, split and duplicate clips, edit the notes inside them, change the tempo, load your own stems, and export the mix as a WAV. Nothing leaves your device.

    Built for an assistant to drive

    Every arrangement change can be written as a short plan: a list of moves such as "clear the drums in the break" or "insert the chorus again at bar 21". The page checks a plan before applying it, applies it whole or not at all, and says what changed in plain words. Copy the brief, give it to Claude with what you want, and paste the plan back. The same thing is available to scripts as window.stemStudio.

    The request box understands a handful of phrasings by itself. It is a small scripted matcher, not an AI model, and it says so when it does not understand.

    Keys

    • Space play or stop · Home back to the start
    • On a clip: ← → move a bar · Shift with arrows resize · D duplicate · S split at the playhead · Delete remove · Enter edit
    • In the note grid: arrows move · Enter or Space toggles a note
    • Ctrl or Cmd + Z undo · with Shift redo

    Stems

    Load stems adds each audio file as its own track from bar 1. Loaded audio plays at its own speed and is not saved in the project file, so load it again after reopening. Download a stem per track renders each synth track on its own.

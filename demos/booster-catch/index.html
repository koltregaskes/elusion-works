About this replay

An original 3D reconstruction of the last 54 seconds of the first Super Heavy tower catch: Starship Flight 5, Booster 12, launched from Starbase, Texas at 7:25 am local time on 13 October 2024. Every model, texture and effect here is procedural and made for this page. No SpaceX footage, models or logos are used.

What is modelled, not measured

Altitude, speed, the exact path and lean, when the engines drop from thirteen to three, how fast the arms close, the layout of the site, the weather and the light. These are a plausible fit between the three known times. Treat the readout as an illustration.

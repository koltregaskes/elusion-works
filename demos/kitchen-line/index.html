Pickleball, singles

Kitchen Line

A quick game on a full-size court, with simplified singles rules (what is left out is under How to play). Get to the ball, pick your shot, and stay out of the kitchen when you volley.

How to play
  • Move: arrow keys or W A S D, or point with the mouse or a finger on your side of the net. Your player runs to where you point.
  • Hit: Space or a click to drive, K to dink it softly over the net, L to lob. The buttons do the same. Swing as the ball arrives: a swing lasts a quarter of a second and takes the ball at its best moment in that time.
  • Aim: the way you are moving, left or right. With the mouse or a finger, point across the net: that aims the shot and leaves you where you stand.
  • Serve: any shot key. It must land in the far court on the diagonal.
  • Two bounces: the serve must bounce before it is returned, and the return must bounce too. After that you may volley.
  • The kitchen: the lighter strip by the net. Never volley while standing in it.
  • Scoring: only the server scores. Lose the rally on your serve and the serve changes hands. Win by two.
  • Left out: doubles, foot faults on the serve, and the fault for being carried into the kitchen by your own volley.
  • Pause: P or Esc. The game also pauses when you look away.

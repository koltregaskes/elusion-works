Still Water

Twelve photographs hang in a set of empty, tiled pool rooms. Nobody else is here. Walk through, wade across the water, and stop where you like, or let the page walk you round.

W A S D or ↑ ↓ walk · ← → turn · drag to look · PageDown next photograph · PageUp the one before · T walk me round · M sound

Drag on the left of the screen to walk. Drag on the right to look round.

The photographs are stand-ins from the Kodak test set, hung here to show the rooms working. After a post by Ben Springer. This is an original build: the rooms, the water and the light were written for this page.

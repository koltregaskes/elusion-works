Last updated: 16 November 2026. This notice covers the Elusion Works studio site and demos. Kol Tregaskes operates the studio. Use the existing contact route for privacy enquiries. It opens a separate site with its own notice.

Hosting and requests

This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives network information such as your IP address and requested URLs and may retain hosting and security logs under its own policies. The studio does not set GitHub's log retention period. Ordinary browser and CDN caches may retain downloaded files; use your browser controls to clear them.

Their retention and international processing are governed by their own policies. A font or script request can occur as soon as the relevant page opens. Listing a provider is not a claim that every page loads it.

Storage and your choices

The studio remembers a theme preference in localStorage. Atmosphere stores display and playback preferences, place and station labels, units and design settings. Older editions accepted unused weather and maps keys without contacting a provider. These may remain in browser storage until cleared or removed by the updated demo. No API credentials are needed; do not enter credentials. You can clear this site's storage in your browser at any time.

Ashfall and Void Sovereign keep game, menu, tutorial, control and audio preferences locally; Last Cartridge keeps archive interaction state. Demo design panels may keep their settings. These values remain until you clear site data or the demo replaces them. Some demos offer image-drop previews; do not supply private images. No studio upload endpoint is implemented for these previews. Browser storage belongs to the origin: project paths on koltregaskes.github.io can share it.

Orbit Census asks for location only after you choose its locate control and the browser grants permission. Its code uses coordinates locally to estimate overhead passes; it does not send those coordinates to CelesTrak. You can deny or revoke permission in your browser. No demo service worker is registered in this source edition; this does not establish what was previously installed in a visitor's browser.

Analytics, forms and contact

No analytics integration, advertising tracker or first-party cookie writer is present in this code edition. Hosting logs and third-party resource requests still occur. Golden and Neon forms are fictional demonstrations and do not send messages. Use the studio contact link for a real enquiry. The separate contact site explains how it handles a submitted enquiry.

Your UK data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK GDPR rights include access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Where consent is the basis, you can withdraw it. Contact the studio using the route above to exercise applicable rights or raise a concern. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. Third-party providers control their own logs and processing; the studio cannot promise to erase records it does not control.

We use local preferences to provide the choices you request and hosting request information to deliver and protect the site. This notice must be reviewed before adding analytics, real forms, new providers or server-side storage. No fixed retention period or international-transfer safeguard is claimed here without provider evidence.
